Approvers / Processing

Guide for approvers and processing (HR, Admin, Payroll)

1. Your PIN

The Administrator sends you a temporary PIN privately (for example by Viber or a phone call). Log in with it, then click Change my PIN and choose your own (4 to 8 digits). Times on the page are Philippine time. Never share it and never write it on the form. If you are on leave, your backup (OIC) uses their own PIN. Forgot your PIN? Ask the Administrator to set a new one. This PIN is separate from your employee login PIN.

4. Approving or processing on the helpdesk

  1. Click Approvers / Processing, type your PIN and click Open.
  2. Step 1 – Waiting for your approval: click Show all details to read the full form. Leave requests show the employee's leave balance. Click Approve, or Reject and type the reason (required).
  3. Step 2 – Waiting for processing (HR, Admin and Payroll accounts): when the work is done, click Mark as processed (you can add a remark, for example "Encoded in the Nov 6–20 payroll"). If a document is missing, click On hold and type what is needed: the employee sees it.
  4. Click Download my list (Excel) if you want everything waiting for you in Excel.
  5. Click Log out when you finish. The page also logs you out after 2 minutes.
Your decision, your name, the date and time are printed on the employee's form as an approval stamp. You do not need to sign that form again.

5. Approving on paper

When you cannot come to the computer, HR prints a List for Approval with all your pending requests. For each line, tick Approve or Reject, write a remark if you reject, then sign at the bottom and return the list to HR. HR records your decisions in the system, and the request then moves to step 2.

1. First-time setup

  1. Copy KUI_Helpdesk.html to the helpdesk PC (for example C:\KUI Helpdesk\). Open it with Chrome or Edge, and always use that same browser. Make a desktop shortcut and press F11 for full screen.
  2. Click 🔒 Administrator. There is no default PIN: type the Administrator's name and position, and create the Administrator PIN (6 to 8 digits), twice. (If you used an older version, your HR PIN is now the Administrator PIN: set your name in Settings → Administrator.)
  3. Write the recovery code shown on the screen and keep it in the HR safe. It is the only way to reset a forgotten Administrator PIN.
  4. Department heads (HOD / Manager): type each approver's name and position. Set the PIN with the approver beside you. Add a backup approver (OIC) when possible.
  5. Department accounts: set the HR, Admin (Dormitory) and Payroll accounts: name, position and PIN of the person in charge, and a backup.
  6. Forms: check the ticket prefix, the next number, who approves (step 1) and which department processes (step 2). "No approval" means the employee only prints the form (default for the Luggage / Item Slip).
  7. Employee list: upload the Masterlist (Emp ID, Name, Position, Department, Hiring Location).
  8. Leave balances: upload the HR working file (section 6 below).
  9. Click Save settings.

3. Transferring the administrator role

  1. The current Administrator opens 🔒 Administrator → Administrator → Transfer the administrator role, with the new administrator beside them.
  2. The current Administrator types their PIN, then the new administrator's name and position. The new administrator types their own PIN twice (the old one looks away).
  3. A new recovery code is shown once: the new administrator writes it down and keeps it in the HR safe. The old PIN and the old recovery code stop working at once. The transfer is recorded in the Administrator history and the Access log.

4. Giving out employee PINs

Easiest way, no need to go to the PC each time: in Administrator settings, click Generate temporary PIN once, write it down and tell it to employees in person. Anyone with no PIN yet logs in with their Employee ID and that PIN, and must change it right away. For forgotten PINs, click Make reset codes (20, 50 or 100), keep the list in the HR office, and give one code (crossed out) to each employee who forgot. They reset the PIN themselves with "Forgot my PIN?" on the login screen. Codes never expire; make more when the count runs low.

Other way, per employee:

  1. In Administrator → Employee PINs, click Temporary PINs for all with no PIN. The helpdesk makes a random PIN for each employee. Show them on screen, or print slips (10 per page) with a receipt list.
  2. If printed, cut the slips. Hand each slip only to its owner and have them sign the receipt list.
  3. At the first login, the employee must choose their own 6 to 8 digit PIN.
  4. Lost slip or forgotten PIN: find the employee in the list and click New temporary PIN (a new random PIN). Locked: click Unlock. If the employee is with you, Employee types PIN lets them set their own PIN right away.
The app keeps only a scrambled code of each PIN. A temporary PIN is shown only on the slip, so it cannot be printed again: make a new one instead.
The data is saved online, so everyone sees the same requests from any computer or phone. Still download a backup now and then.
Printing: by default employees only submit, and HR prints the forms. Any Print or Reprint button can also Save as PDF (for example to the HR USB) to print on another computer. To let employees print their own copy at this PC, tick "Employees print their own forms" in Administrator settings. Temporary PINs can be shown on screen instead of printed.

5. Every day

  1. Open 🔒 Ticket Log with the Administrator or HR PIN. Filter by request type, approval, processing status or name.
  2. Print the list for approval: pick one approver or "All approvers" (one page each). Tick "Also print each request form" if the approver needs the full forms.
  3. When the signed list comes back, set the Approval column of each ticket to Approved or Rejected (type the reason). The method is saved as "Signed approval list", and approved requests move to step 2.
  4. Step 2: HR, Admin and Payroll mark their requests Processed on Approvers / Processing (or HR does it in the Processing column of the Ticket Log). Filter Processing "For processing" to see what is still open.
  5. Set the HR status as the papers come in: Open, Signed copy received, Encoded in HR file, Cancelled.
  6. Use Reprint for one form, or filter HR status "Open" and click Print / save as PDF: forms shown below to print all new forms at once. Employees sign them at the HR office; then set HR status to "Signed copy received".

6. Leave balances

  1. Keep the HR working file up to date as usual (Leave Requests, validation, Leave Balances).
  2. In 🔒 Administrator → Leave balances, click Upload HR working file and choose the .xlsx file. The helpdesk reads the Leave Balances sheet (entitlement, earned, carried over, used and balance per employee) and the Balances as of date on the Setup sheet. Check the preview, then click Replace leave balances.
  3. Employees see their entitlement, earned, carried over, used and balance in My Leave Balance. Leave they file in the helpdesk after the "as of" date is shown separately as not yet counted, with an estimated balance. The leave form warns when a leave is more than the balance, and the approver sees the note.
  4. Upload again after each update of the HR file (for example every cut-off), so the balances stay current.

7. Excel files

  1. In the Ticket Log, under Download Excel, choose the approval status (for example Pending approval or Approved), the request type and the dates filed.
  2. Tick Only not yet downloaded for each cut-off, so nothing is encoded twice.
  3. Click Download Excel (.xlsx). The file has a Summary sheet, an All requests sheet and one sheet per request type with every detail.
  4. Leave: paste into the Leave Requests sheet of the HR working file. Manual entries: into Approved Manual Entries of the sorted biometric. Change of Schedule: update the roster and the Roster Changes sheet. Overtime and Salary Concerns: to the payroll file and the Timekeeping Concerns Log. Then set HR status to "Encoded in HR file" and mark the request Processed.

8. Storage and backup

The Ticket Log and the Home page show how much storage is used and when the last backup was made. A yellow Backup needed note appears when the last backup is older than the number of days set in Administrator settings (7 by default), when many new tickets were filed since, or when storage is above 80%.

  1. Click Backup now to save a .json file. Copy it to a USB or the HR drive.
  2. On a new PC (or after the browser was reset), open the app, create the Administrator account, then click Restore and choose the backup file. Employee PINs, accounts and leave balances come back too.
  3. To install a new version of the app, back up first, then replace the .html file in the same folder with the same name. The tickets and PINs stay.

10. Capacity

The saved data is packed (compressed) before it is stored, so this browser can hold many times more tickets than before: the Ticket Log shows the exact room left. Above 80%, Backup and click Archive old tickets (encoded or cancelled, older than 60 days). Ticket numbers have no limit: after 9999 they continue to 10000, and a number is never reused.

How to use the Helpdesk

Guide for employees. Approvers, HR and the Administrator find their guides inside their own pages, after their PIN.

1. What the helpdesk is for

Use the helpdesk to file your requests to HR, Payroll and Admin, from a computer or a phone. You log in with your Employee ID and your own PIN, so nobody else can file a request under your name. Each request gets a ticket number.

2. Your PIN

  1. Get the temporary PIN from HR.
  2. Click any request (or Log in on Home). Type your Employee ID and the temporary PIN.
  3. Choose your own PIN of 6 to 8 digits and type it twice. Do not use 123456, 111111 or your birthday.
  4. Never share your PIN. Whatever is filed with your login is your responsibility.
After 5 wrong PINs your login locks for 15 minutes. Forgot your PIN? Ask HR for a reset code, click Forgot my PIN? on the login screen, and choose a new PIN. To change your PIN, log in and click Change my PIN.

4. Filing a request

  1. On Home, click the request you need, then log in.
  2. Check the blue box: your name, position, department and who will approve. If something is wrong, tell HR before you continue.
  3. Read the yellow filing reminder. It tells you if you are filing on time.
  4. Fill in all the fields with a red *. Write the real times and dates.
  5. For a Change of Schedule, choose your counterpart's name, or Not applicable if there is no swap. Your counterpart must agree on the helpdesk before your request goes to the approver: either they type their own PIN beside you now, or they agree the next time they log in. They also sign the Conforme on the printed form.
  6. Tick the certification box, then click Submit. Your request goes to the approver (for a swap: after your counterpart agrees). Once approved, it goes to HR, Admin or Payroll for processing.
  7. Write down or take a photo of your ticket number. HR prints your form; sign it at the HR office and give the documents asked for (below).
  8. Click Done – log out. If you walk away, you are logged out after 3 minutes.
Left the form by mistake? Click ← Back (top left) or your browser's back button any time. What you typed is kept for 3 days: log in again and open the same request, and it comes back. Click Start over to clear it. Only you can see your unfinished form, and it is cleared once you submit.
If the screen shows a yellow note after you submit (for example "filed late"), your request is still saved. The approver will see the note.

7. Your leave balance

Log in and click My Leave Balance. You see your entitlement for the contract period (Annual Leave, or Vacation and Sick Leave), how much you have earned so far (leave is earned monthly), what was carried over, how much you used, and your balance, as of the date HR last updated it. Leave you filed in the helpdesk after that date is shown separately, with an estimated balance. When you file a leave, the form shows your balance and warns you if the leave is more than your balance.
